Privacy Policy

ColorFlow: Puzzle Quest

Effective and last updated: November 19, 2026

FikraPlay Studio ("we", "us") develops ColorFlow: Puzzle Quest, Android package com.fikraplay.colorflow ("the game"). This policy explains how information is handled in the game and on this privacy-policy website.

The game is intended for players aged 8 and older, including children. It does not require an account. Game progress and a broad age-group choice are stored on your device.

The game shows ads through Google AdMob to all age groups, but only after the player answers the age-group screen. Players under 18, and players who do not provide an age group, receive only non-personalized ads rated suitable for all audiences. Players under 13, or who do not provide an age group, are treated as children: their ad requests are marked as child-directed under the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). Personalized ads are possible only for players who select 18 or older, subject to applicable consent choices.

1. Information handled by the game

On-device information. The game saves progress, completed levels, stars, coins, hints, achievements, themes, daily challenges, unfinished puzzles and settings in app storage. The age screen stores only a selected age group (under 13, 13–17, 18 or older, or not provided), rather than a date of birth. We do not upload this save data to a FikraPlay Studio server or operate a cloud-save service.

The game does not ask for your name, email address or account credentials. It does not access your contacts, camera, microphone, photos or precise device location. There is no account registration, chat, in-app purchasing, or separate Firebase analytics or crash-reporting service in this version. AdMob has its own advertising measurement and diagnostics, described below.

Information you send to us. If you email support, we receive your email address, message and any information you choose to include. We use it to respond to questions, resolve issues and handle privacy requests. Please do not send passwords, identity documents or unnecessary personal information.

2. Google AdMob advertising

The game uses Google's Mobile Ads SDK to display rewarded video ads (which players choose to watch for a hint, extra moves or a reward) and interstitial ads between levels. The SDK does not start, and no ad is requested, until the age-group screen has been answered. Ad settings then depend on the selected age group:

  • Under 13, or age not provided: treated as a child. Ad requests are tagged as child-directed (COPPA) and under the age of consent, personalized and interest-based advertising is turned off, and ads are limited to content rated for all audiences (G).
  • 13–17: ad requests use Google's teen treatment and are tagged as under the age of consent, personalized advertising is turned off, and ads are limited to content rated for all audiences (G).
  • 18 or older: standard advertising, which may be personalized where permitted by consent and other applicable settings, limited to content rated for general audiences with parental guidance (PG).

Google applies its own policies to child-directed and under-age-of-consent requests, including limits on the identifiers and data it uses for them. We use only Google AdMob, which participates in Google Play's Families Self-Certified Ads SDK Program. Depending on the age group, device availability and applicable privacy choices, Google collects and shares:

  • IP address and approximate location: an IP address may be used to infer a general location for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. This is distinct from GPS or precise-location permission.
  • Device and other identifiers: such as an Android advertising ID, app-set ID and other identifiers available to the SDK.
  • App and ad interactions: such as app launches, ad views, taps and video interactions, used for advertising, reporting and measurement.
  • Diagnostics and performance information: such as SDK performance, app-launch time, crashes or hangs, used for service operation, analytics and reliability.

Google processes this information for advertising, advertising analytics and fraud prevention. Ads are personalized only for players who select 18 or older, where permitted and subject to consent and other applicable settings. For younger or undeclared players, Google limits processing according to its child-directed and under-age-of-consent policies. Information can be processed by Google and its advertising service providers or partners under their policies. We do not operate our own player-profile database.

Read Google's Privacy Policy, how Google uses information from partner apps, and Google Mobile Ads' data-disclosure information.

3. Privacy choices and permissions

Before the advertising SDK starts, Google's User Messaging Platform checks whether a privacy message is required. Players under 18, or who do not provide an age group, are tagged as under the age of consent, so they are not asked for advertising consent and receive non-personalized ads only. Adults may be shown a consent message where required. Where the consent platform requires it, you can revisit Settings → Ad & Privacy Choices in the game. The availability of ads depends on these choices and service availability; the core puzzles remain playable without ads.

You can also manage or delete the advertising ID through your Android device's privacy or advertising settings, and review Google's ad controls. Resetting an identifier does not itself erase all information previously processed by Google.

The game uses network access for advertising and optional vibration for feedback. It does not request camera, microphone, contacts or precise-location access. The Android advertising-ID permission is included by the ad SDK; for children's ad requests, Google limits the use of identifiers under its child-directed policies.

4. Children and families

The intended audience includes children aged 8–12 as well as older players. The age-group screen presents neutral choices without preselecting an answer. Players can choose not to provide their age group and continue playing; they are then treated as children for advertising. Ads shown to players under 18 are non-personalized and limited to content rated for all audiences, and ads for children are tagged as child-directed. Ads cannot be removed by an in-app purchase; rewarded ads are optional. An age-group choice is not a request for parental consent.

Parents or guardians may contact Laithhn@gmail.com about a child's privacy, to ask about information held by us, or to request its deletion. We do not ask children to send personal information to support. If a child has sent personal information to us, a parent or guardian can request that we delete it.

5. Sharing, international processing and security

Advertising data is transmitted to Google as described above. Support messages are processed using our email service provider. We may disclose information we hold when required by law or when necessary to protect security and legal rights. We do not sell personal information ourselves; Google's advertising data processing is described in its linked policies.

Google and our service providers may process information on servers outside your country. Google states that Mobile Ads SDK data is encrypted in transit using TLS. Local game data is stored in the app's storage area. We take reasonable measures to protect support correspondence and limit its use to the purposes described here. No system can guarantee absolute security.

6. Data retention and deletion

  • Local game data: kept while stored on your device. Settings → Reset Progress resets gameplay progress while keeping settings, including the age-group choice. To remove all app data, including settings and age group, use Android's Clear storage/Clear data control for ColorFlow. Uninstalling normally removes local app data; Android backup or restore may retain or restore data according to your device settings.
  • Support correspondence: retained as long as needed to resolve the request and meet applicable legal, security or dispute-resolution obligations, then deleted when no longer necessary. You may request earlier deletion, subject to those obligations.
  • Google advertising data: retained according to Google's purposes and retention rules. See Google's retention policy and Google's privacy controls. Clearing the game does not automatically delete data already processed by Google.

There are no game accounts to delete. To request access, correction or deletion of personal information we hold, or to raise a privacy objection, email the address below with the game name and your request. Depending on your location, you may have additional privacy rights, including a right to complain to your data-protection authority. We may request only information reasonably needed to verify and process your request. We cannot directly identify or delete every record independently controlled by Google, but can help direct you to its privacy tools.
