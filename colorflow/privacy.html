Privacy Policy

ColorFlow: Puzzle Quest

Effective and last updated: November 24, 2026

FikraPlay Studio ("we", "us") develops ColorFlow: Puzzle Quest, Android package com.fikraplay.colorflow ("the game"). This policy applies to the current version of the game and explains how information is handled in the game and on this privacy-policy website.

The game is intended for children and older players. It does not require an account. Game progress and settings are stored on your device. The game does not ask for or save your age.

The game shows ads through Google AdMob with the same settings for everyone: child-directed treatment, personalized advertising disabled, and a maximum ad content rating of G. There is no age question or age setting. Optional in-app purchases are processed by Google Play.

1. Information handled by the game

On-device information. The game saves progress, completed levels, stars, coins, hints, achievements, themes, daily challenges, unfinished puzzles and settings in app storage. The game does not collect or store an age group or date of birth. We do not upload this save data to a FikraPlay Studio server or operate a cloud-save service.

The game does not ask for your name, email address or account credentials. It does not access your contacts, camera, microphone, photos or precise device location. There is no account registration, chat, or separate Firebase analytics or crash-reporting service in this version. AdMob has its own advertising measurement and diagnostics, described below.

In-app purchases. Optional purchases (Remove Ads, hint and coin packs) are processed by Google Play; FikraPlay Studio does not receive your payment-card details. The game stores on your device which items you own and Google's order identifiers, so a purchase is delivered exactly once and Remove Ads can be restored. The purchase library (Unity In-App Purchasing) sends transaction details, app and device identifiers, device information and an approximate country derived from the IP address to Unity Technologies to operate purchases and prevent duplicate processing. These operational events may be sent even when advertising and analytics consent is denied. The game sets both consent signals to denied; Unity states that advertising identifiers are not collected in that state. See Unity's Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

Information you send to us. If you email support, we receive your email address, message and any information you choose to include. We use it to respond to questions, resolve issues and handle privacy requests. Please do not send passwords, identity documents or unnecessary personal information.

2. Google AdMob advertising

The game uses Google's Mobile Ads SDK to display rewarded video ads (which players choose to watch for a hint, extra moves or a reward), interstitial ads between levels, and banner ads at the bottom of menu screens (never during a puzzle). The same safeguards apply to all players:

  • All ad requests are marked as child-directed and configured with Google's G (general audiences) content-rating filter.
  • Personalized and interest-based advertising is disabled.
  • The consent SDK is configured for under-age treatment. No age question or age confirmation is shown or saved.

Google applies its own policies to child-directed and under-age-of-consent requests, including limits on the identifiers and data it uses for them. We use only Google AdMob, which participates in Google Play's Families Self-Certified Ads SDK Program. Subject to its child-directed restrictions and device availability, Google may process:

  • IP address and approximate location: an IP address may be used to infer a general location for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. This is distinct from GPS or precise-location permission.
  • Identifiers: Google may process identifiers permitted under its child-directed restrictions. Google's child-directed treatment prevents transmission of the Android advertising ID.
  • App and ad interactions: such as app launches, ad views, taps and video interactions, used for advertising, reporting and measurement.
  • Diagnostics and performance information: such as SDK performance, app-launch time, crashes or hangs, used for service operation, analytics and reliability.

Google processes this information for advertising, advertising analytics and fraud prevention. Personalized advertising is disabled for all players, and requests use Google's child-directed restrictions. Information can be processed by Google and its advertising service providers or partners under their policies. We do not operate our own player-profile database.

Read Google's Privacy Policy, how Google uses information from partner apps, and Google Mobile Ads' data-disclosure information.

3. Privacy choices and permissions

The advertising SDK starts only after the child-directed and G-rated request settings are applied. Google's User Messaging Platform is configured for under-age treatment, which suppresses the advertising consent form. Personalized ads remain disabled for everyone. Where the consent platform requires a privacy-options entry point, it is available in Settings → Ad & Privacy Choices. The core puzzles remain playable without ads.

You can also manage or delete the advertising ID through your Android device's privacy or advertising settings, and review Google's ad controls. Resetting an identifier does not itself erase all information previously processed by Google.

The game uses network access for advertising, Google Play's billing permission for optional in-app purchases, and optional vibration for feedback. It does not request camera, microphone, contacts or precise-location access. The Android advertising-ID permission is included by the ad SDK; for children's ad requests, Google limits the use of identifiers under its child-directed policies.

4. Children and families

The intended audience includes children and older players. The same child-directed, non-personalized advertising settings and G-rating filter apply to everyone without an age question. Banner ads and ads between levels can be removed with the optional Remove Ads purchase; rewarded ads are always optional.

Parents or guardians may contact FikraPlayStudio@gmail.com about a child's privacy, to ask about information held by us, or to request its deletion. We do not ask children to send personal information to support. If a child has sent personal information to us, a parent or guardian can request that we delete it.

5. Sharing, international processing and security

Advertising data is transmitted to Google and operational purchase information to Unity as described above. Google Play processes purchases under its own policies. Support messages are processed using our email service provider. We may disclose information we hold when required by law or when necessary to protect security and legal rights. We do not sell personal information ourselves; Google's advertising data processing is described in its linked policies.

Google, Unity and our service providers may process information on servers outside your country. Google states that Mobile Ads SDK data is encrypted in transit using TLS. Local game data is stored in the app's storage area. We take reasonable measures to protect support correspondence and limit its use to the purposes described here. No system can guarantee absolute security.

6. Data retention and deletion

  • Local game data: kept while stored on your device. Settings → Reset Progress resets gameplay progress while keeping settings. The game does not store an age group. To remove all app data, use Android's Clear storage/Clear data control for ColorFlow. Purchases remain associated with your Google Play account; eligible non-consumable purchases can be restored through the game. Uninstalling normally removes local app data; Android backup or restore may retain or restore data according to your device settings.
  • Support correspondence: retained as long as needed to resolve the request and meet applicable legal, security or dispute-resolution obligations, then deleted when no longer necessary. You may request earlier deletion, subject to those obligations.
  • Purchase records: Google Play and Unity retain transaction records for purchase delivery, restoration, reporting and their applicable retention obligations. Clearing local app data does not erase their records. Contact us about access or deletion requests involving Unity purchase processing.
  • Google advertising data: retained according to Google's purposes and retention rules. See Google's retention policy and Google's privacy controls. Clearing the game does not automatically delete data already processed by Google.

There are no game accounts to delete. To request access, correction or deletion of personal information we hold, or to raise a privacy objection, email the address below with the game name and your request. Depending on your location, you may have additional privacy rights, including a right to complain to your data-protection authority. We may request only information reasonably needed to verify and process your request. We cannot directly identify or delete every record independently controlled by Google, but can help direct you to its privacy tools.
